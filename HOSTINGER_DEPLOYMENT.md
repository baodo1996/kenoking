# 🚀 Keno Game Deployment Guide for keno-king.bet 
 
## 📋 Hostinger Deployment Steps 
 
### Step 1: Access Hostinger Control Panel 
1. Log in to your Hostinger account 
2. Go to **Websites** section 
3. Click **Manage** next to keno-king.bet 
 
### Step 2: Upload Files 
1. Click **File Manager** in the left sidebar 
2. Navigate to **public_html** folder 
3. Upload these files from the production folder: 
   - index.html (upload to root of public_html) 
   - .htaccess (upload to root of public_html) 
   - robots.txt (upload to root of public_html) 
   - sitemap.xml (upload to root of public_html) 
 
### Step 3: Enable SSL Certificate 
1. Go to **SSL** section in Hostinger panel 
2. Enable SSL for keno-king.bet 
3. Wait for SSL activation (usually instant) 
 
### Step 4: Test Your Game 
1. Visit https://keno-king.bet 
2. Test all game features 
3. Test on mobile devices
